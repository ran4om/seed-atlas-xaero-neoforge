The generation engine and map icons come from DUzzL's Seed Atlas project,
which is derived from Cubitect's Cubiomes Viewer. This mod uses Seed Atlas
commit fc2c628693bc72130d7c16a21636f50d57553ddf. Original copyright notices
remain in effect.

This is an unofficial port of DUzzL's seed-atlas-xaero to Minecraft 1.21.1 and
NeoForge, made in October 2026. The port changes the Java integration for the
1.21.1 and NeoForge APIs, binds the native library through JNA, and patches the
engine build for two 1.21.1 vanilla differences (End biome borders and the
stronghold biome tag). The port is distributed under GPLv3 without warranty.

This is an independent extension, not an official Xaero release. It is not
an official Minecraft product and is not approved by Mojang or Microsoft.
